Services

Common Ways I Can Help

PsychoPy and Pavlovia Programming

I offer a wide range of help in programming PsychoPy and Pavlovia experiments. From simple bug fixes or adding new features to designing entire experiments that can run locally or online, I can help. I also make an effort to heavily comment any custom code so that things are clear and easy to follow.

Data Processing and Analyses (Behavioural and Neuroimaging)

I can assist with data analyses and develop scripts or pipelines to help process data in Python or R. I have experience working with complex behavioural, physiological, and neuroimaging data including fMRI and EEG.

Custom Research Software

I can also build custom software that goes beyond PsychoPy, including web apps, browser-based experiments, small research tools, data-entry systems, dashboards, or phone-friendly apps for collecting responses and running lightweight study workflows.

General Programming Help

I have years of experience designing experiments in a variety of languages including E-Prime, MATLAB, and C++. I have programmed experiments from the ground up for behavioural and neuroimaging research projects.
